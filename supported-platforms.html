Supported Platforms

Zapt (also called Zaptdl) is a Telegram media downloader bot

Zapt works with many video and media pages through yt-dlp. Popular services include the following; actual support depends on the individual link and whether the source is accessible.

Twitch

Some Twitch media links may be available, depending on the source and access restrictions.

Reddit

Some Reddit video and media links can be processed when reachable.

SoundCloud

Supported SoundCloud links may provide audio downloads through the bot.

Vimeo

Some publicly accessible Vimeo media links can be handled by Zapt.

And many more

Zapt uses yt-dlp, which supports a broad range of media websites. The repository describes coverage as 1,000+ sites, but no single list guarantees every link works. Site updates, private content, regional access, sign-in requirements, age restrictions, and changing source rules can affect results.

For best results, send a direct link to a public video or media page. Do not share private cookies or credentials with the bot.
