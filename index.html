A Telegram video downloader for links you already have

Zapt is a Telegram media downloader bot built for a simple workflow: share a link from a supported public media page, choose a quality when the bot offers one, and receive the download in Telegram. It supports popular services such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, plus many more sites through yt-dlp.
