What is Zapt?

Zapt is a Telegram bot that processes supported media links and sends the resulting media in your chat. It is an open-source project.

How do I download a video with Zapt?

Open the bot, send a link to a supported media page, choose an available quality if prompted, and wait for the bot to send the media. See the step-by-step guide.

Which sites does Zapt support?

Popular sources include YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Twitch, Reddit, SoundCloud and Vimeo, with many more yt-dlp-supported sites. Support varies for individual links. See the platform list.

Is Zapt free?

Yes, Zapt is free to use. Optional premium features are offered via Telegram Stars.

Does Zapt download SD, HD and 4K?

The bot offers SD and HD choices where available. Premium unlocks 4K options when the source has 4K media accessible to the downloader.

Is every download fast?

Zapt is designed to process downloads efficiently, but time depends on the source website, queue load, file size and network conditions. A specific completion time is not guaranteed.

Why did my link fail?

The link may be private, removed, restricted, inaccessible in your region, require sign-in, or no longer be supported because the source changed. Telegram file limits, formats and connectivity can also affect delivery.

Can I download private posts?

Private and login-restricted content may not be accessible to Zapt. Never share your account password or private cookies with the bot.

Can I download and share any video?

Only download or share media when you have the rights and permission to do so. Respect copyright and the source platform's terms.

Where do I report a problem or see the source?

Visit the GitHub repository for project source, issues and documentation, or open the bot to try a supported link.
